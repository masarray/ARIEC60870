Troubleshooting

IEC 60870 Common Address mismatch: the device answers, but the ASDU belongs somewhere else.

CA mismatch is one of the most confusing field symptoms: the physical link or IEC-104 TCP session can look healthy, but application data does not match the expected station. ARIEC60870 keeps the raw TX/RX frame visible and explains the decoded CA so the issue is easier to prove.

Why it happens

The transport can be correct while the ASDU address is wrong.

IEC 101 and IEC 104 separate link/session reachability from application addressing. A device can accept communication while sending an ASDU Common Address that does not match the engineering profile, RTU station, bay controller, or test setup.
