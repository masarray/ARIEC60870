What it means

Smart Findings are not magic. They are evidence summaries.

A finding should not hide the protocol. It should summarize the symptom, point to the frame or counter that proves it, explain why the symptom matters, and suggest a practical next check.

ProblemA short statement of what looks wrong: CA mismatch, GI incomplete, ACTCON missing, bad quality, unknown IOA, or unstable session.
ProofThe raw frame, address, COT, sequence counter, quality flag, event, or timeout pattern behind the finding.
Likely causeThe engineering reason the symptom usually happens: wrong CA, mapping mismatch, unsupported command, busy link, or missing report flow.
Next stepA practical check: verify address, re-run GI, inspect IOA mapping, check ACTCON/ACTTERM, compare sequence counters, or export report evidence.
