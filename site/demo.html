Why the demo matters.

ARIEC60870 is designed to make IEC 60870 testing easier to explain, easier to learn, and easier to document.

1. TestRun an authorized IEC 101, IEC 103, or IEC 104 session using project-approved communication settings.
2. InterpretUse the trace workspace to keep raw TX/RX evidence visible while reading selected-frame meaning.
3. DiagnoseUse Smart Findings to connect CA mismatch, GI gaps, command confirmation issues, quality flags, or session symptoms to proof and next steps.
4. ReportGenerate a native PDF evidence report for FAT, SAT, commissioning, troubleshooting, homologation support, certification attachment, vendor comparison, or handover.
