Protocol coverage

IEC-101, IEC-103, and IEC-104 workflows with one evidence model.

ARIEC60870 is designed as a single-endpoint evidence analyzer. It focuses on visibility, repeatable testing, and readable output rather than replacing a full SCADA master station.

Download latest release Open GitHub repository

IEC 60870-5-101

Serial telecontrol workflow with FT1.2 frame transparency and practical master-side evidence.

  • Fixed and variable FT1.2 frame visibility.
  • General Interrogation and optional clock sync.
  • Type ID, COT, CA, IOA, value, quality, and timestamp evidence.

IEC 60870-5-103

Protection relay serial workflow for Class 1/Class 2 polling and relay event review.

  • Reset link and FCB handling.
  • ACD/DFC visibility and controlled event drain.
  • FUN, INF, DPI, measurand, relay timestamp, and mapping profile support.

IEC 60870-5-104

TCP client workflow for APCI/APDU visibility and session-level troubleshooting.

  • STARTDT, STOPDT, TESTFR, I/S/U frame visibility.
  • Sequence counter evidence.
  • Common ASDU decode and reportable findings.

Product boundary

The app is intentionally focused and transparent.

  • One active endpoint/session at a time.
  • Not a vendor-specific relay database.
  • Not a redundant production master station or formal FAT/SAT replacement.
