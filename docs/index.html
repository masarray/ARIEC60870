Why it is different

From raw frames to clear engineering evidence.

Communication can look healthy even when data or command feedback is missing. ArSee connects original frames, decoded values, events, findings, and reports so engineers can inspect what happened.

Protocol testerCheck IEC-101 serial, IEC-103 relay, and IEC-104 TCP/IP communication in an authorized engineering session.
Event to original-frame evidenceInspect interpreted TX/RX and follow Event Journal evidence to captured original frames. Missing source TX is marked incomplete, never fabricated.
Smart FindingsTurn CA mismatch, GI gaps, command timeout, missing ACTCON/ACTTERM, or unstable IEC-104 sessions into problem, proof, and next step.
Evidence reportGenerate native PDF evidence for FAT, SAT, commissioning, troubleshooting, homologation support, vendor comparison, and handover.
