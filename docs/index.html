Feature overview

Capabilities from connection to evidence.

Explore supported IEC-101/103/104 engineering workflows. Availability varies by protocol mode, device profile and published release.

IEC-101 master & redundancy

  • Active unbalanced FT1.2 serial sessions, Class 1/2 polling and startup General Interrogation.
  • Dual-link A/B health, active/standby ownership, switchover timeline and supported control evidence.

IEC-101 passive monitoring

  • Receive-only serial observation with supported multi-RX workflows.
  • Capture raw traffic without intentional transmit; electrical isolation depends on hardware.

IEC-103 protection relays

  • Serial relay link exchanges, supported FUN/INF decoding, values and protection events.
  • Read relay evidence with associated protocol time and quality where supplied.

IEC-104 TCP/IP

  • Client/master sessions with APCI I/S/U frames, sequence context and STARTDT/STOPDT/TESTFR.
  • Decode ASDU values/events and review supported GI and command transactions.

Frame Trace & diagnostics

  • Original TX/RX bytes, Type ID, COT, CA, IOA, link functions and quality flags.
  • Inspect anomalous responses, missing GI milestones and session behavior without hiding raw evidence.

Values, SOE & Signal List

  • Current point values and retained Event Journal history are distinct views.
  • User-defined signal names, state maps and Factor or X0/X100/Y0/Y100 engineering scaling.

GI, controls & Smart Findings

  • Review requests, ACTCON, data, ACTTERM, timeouts, negative responses and source-linked findings.
  • Command confirmations are protocol evidence, not a claim of physical equipment operation.

Capture, PDF & learning

  • Save/reopen supported captures, export trace/events and prepare native PDF reports.
  • Bilingual field handbook, Quick Start, example traces and versioned Windows installer/portable packages.

For mode-specific parameters, procedures and boundaries, see Field Wiki and Protocol Coverage. ArSee is an engineering tool, not a production SCADA master or an IEC certification suite.
