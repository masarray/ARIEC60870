## Strategic direction

ARIEC60870 is positioned as a focused IEC 60870 evidence analyzer and commissioning/troubleshooting assistant.

The roadmap intentionally avoids overclaiming a universal protocol test-set replacement and instead focuses on the evidence workflow that can be shown publicly soon:

1. stable line monitor UX,
2. unified evidence capture,
3. PDF evidence export,
4. capture replay/review,
5. trigger/pre-post capture,
6. task mode lite.
